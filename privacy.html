Privacy Policy

App: Editor (video & photo editor, version 1.6) · Package: com.editor_v.myapp
Last updated: 28 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Editor app ("the app", "we") handles. We keep it minimal: your videos and photos are edited on your device and are not uploaded to us.

1. Your media (videos, photos, audio)

Files you pick are processed locally on your device for editing and export. We do not upload, store or view your media. Exported videos are saved to your device only; sharing them is your choice. The app asks for access to your photos/videos/audio only through the system file picker when you add media.

2. Account information

If you sign in, we store your email address and your account's subscription status on our server (hosted on Render, database on MongoDB). Passwords are stored in hashed form. Email is used only for sign-in, one-time passcodes and subscription management. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

3. Payments

Paid plans (Pro) are processed by Razorpay. Payment details (card, UPI, bank) are entered on Razorpay's secure page and are never seen or stored by us. We receive only a payment confirmation and plan validity dates. See Razorpay's privacy policy.

4. Optional online features

  • Music search sends your search words to Jamendo (jamendo.com) to list royalty-free tracks.
  • AI Studio sends the text prompt you write (and, only if you choose "My media + AI", the selected image) to an AI image/video generation service (e.g. Pollinations) to create content.
  • Cut-out / person detection loads a model from Google's MediaPipe hosting; processing happens on your device.
  • Fonts may be loaded from Google Fonts.

These services receive standard technical data such as your IP address under their own policies. Do not enter personal information in prompts.

5. Data stored on your device

Projects, settings and a sign-in token are kept in the app's local storage on your device. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes them.

6. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad trackers.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data for marketing.
  • No collection of location, contacts, or microphone/camera recordings by us.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

8. Retention and deletion

Account data is kept while your account is active. To request deletion of your account and data, email the address below from your registered email; we will process it within 30 days.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. No method is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change and the latest version will always be at this page.
